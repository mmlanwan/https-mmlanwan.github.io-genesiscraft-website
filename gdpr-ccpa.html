GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Genesis Craft AI respects your privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Your Rights (GDPR – EEA residents)

  • Right to access – request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification – correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) – delete your data.
  • Right to restrict processing – limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability – receive your data in a machine‑readable format.
  • Right to object – stop certain processing (e.g., direct marketing).

Your Rights (CCPA – California residents)

  • Right to know – categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to delete – deletion of personal information (subject to exceptions).
  • Right to opt‑out – of sale of personal information (we do not sell your data).
  • Right to non‑discrimination – equal service and price regardless of privacy choices.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit a request via e-mail to privacy@genesiscraft.ai. We will respond within 30 days. You may also designate an authorized agent to act on your behalf.

Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations.

International Transfers

We may transfer data to countries outside the EEA (including the US) under Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
