We're Building the Future of Software Development

Genesis Craft AI was founded on a simple belief: everyone should be able to create software. We're a team of AI researchers, developer tools veterans, and passionate engineers on a mission to democratize application development.

Join Our Mission →

From Idea to Impact

What started as a research project at Xerox Corporation became a mission to transform how software is built.
2021

💡 The Insight

Our research showed that 70% of developer time is spent on repetitive tasks, not creative problem-solving. We wondered: what if AI could handle the boilerplate?

2023

⚙️ The Prototype

We built the first version of our multi-agent system. It could generate simple React components from natural language — but we knew it could do more.

2025

🚀 The Breakthrough

After 18 months of R&D, our five-layer validation engine achieved 92% success rate on complex applications. We knew we had something special.

2026

🏢 The Company

We incorporated Genesis Craft AI, assembled a founding team of 4, and launched a private beta with 500 developers. Our mission to democratize software development began in earnest.

2026

🌍 The Launch

Today, thousands of developers use Genesis Craft AI to build faster, better, and more creatively. Our team has grown to 9, and we’re just getting started.

What We Believe

“To democratize software creation by making full-stack application development accessible to everyone — from indie developers to global enterprises.”

Developer First

We build for developers because we are developers. Every decision starts with: 'Does this make development better?'

Quality Above All

We don't generate code — we generate production-ready code. Our 5-layer validation ensures security, performance, and best practices.

Radical Transparency

We're open about our capabilities and limitations. We share roadmap, research, and learnings with the community.

AI for Empowerment

AI should augment human creativity, not replace it. We handle repetitive work so developers can focus on the innovative.

Customer-Obsessed

Your success is our success. We measure everything by whether we've helped you build faster, better, and more joyfully.
